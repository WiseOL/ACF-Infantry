# Heavy weapon movement update

M1919 (weapon_acf_30cal) and M249 now cap walking, sprinting, and slow walking at 85% of current player speed while equipped. Native crouching applies on top of the reduced cap. EPO speed changes are included. Other held weapons, vehicle driving, noclip, and jump power are unaffected. No persistent player speed settings are overwritten.

Anti-material rifle now uses the native 27 mm Autocannon APCR builder with 4.05 cm projectile and 17.55 cm propellant inputs. Native VSS conversion retains both lengths, calculates a 20.25 mm penetrator, approximately 0.103044 kg projectile mass, 1411.29 m/s muzzle velocity, and 65.77 mm normal-incidence muzzle penetration with the inspected defaults. Existing rifle magazine, fire rate, scope, recoil, and model remain unchanged.

Rebar crossbow now uses native 23 mm Cannon APCR with 3.45 cm projectile and 14.95 cm propellant inputs. VSS conversion retains both lengths and calculates a 17.25 mm penetrator, approximately 0.063696 kg projectile mass, 1411.29 m/s muzzle velocity, and 56.03 mm normal-incidence muzzle penetration using inspected defaults. Existing crossbow magazine, reload behavior, accuracy, recoil, and model remain unchanged. Range, impact angle, armor and server settings affect actual penetration.
